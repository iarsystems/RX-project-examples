* Description  : Demonstration of the capacitive touch sensing unit (CTSU). If touch key or touch slider on the board

* Operating Procedure:
*                For details of Building and Downloading the sample code refer to the RSK Tutorial Manual.
*             1. Compile and download the sample code. Click the 'Resume' button to start program execution.
*                Click 'Resume' again if the program stops at main().
*                The LCD display will show 'Touch Demo'.
*
*             2. Touch the touch key on the board. LCD will indicate by 'ON' according to the touched key.
*                LED output will also be then interlocked with it.
*
*             3. Touch and slide the touch slider on the board. LCD will slide a indicate bar to right and
*                left according to a slider position.
*                LED output will also be then interlocked with it.

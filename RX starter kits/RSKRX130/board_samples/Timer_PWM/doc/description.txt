*
*             3. A 1KHz PWM output increasing from 10% to 90% then stepping

*
*             4. Press SW1 to stop the duty cycle modulation and display the duty as a percentage.
*                Observe the static waveform on the oscilloscope.
*
*             5. Press SW1 to continue duty cycle modulation.Observe the waveform's duty cycle varying.

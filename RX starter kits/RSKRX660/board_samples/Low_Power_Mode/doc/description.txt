/***********************************************************************************************************************
* File Name    : description.txt
*
* System Requirements: 
* Device(s)               : R5F56609HxFB
* Tool-Chain              : CC-RX V3.04.00
* Development environment : Renesas CS+ V8.08.00
* Version                 : Renesas Smart Configurator V2.14.0
* H/W Platform            : RSKRX660
*
* Description  : Demonstrates the low power modes SLEEP, DEEP SLEEP  and SW STANDBY for reducing current consumption.
*                
*                
*                NORMAL - Normal operation with peripheral operation and all clocks running.
* 
*                SLEEP - In sleep mode, the CPU stops operating but the contents of its internal registers are retained.
*                Other peripheral functions do not stop.
*                When the WDT is used, the WDT stops counting when sleep mode is entered.
*                
*                DEEP SLEEP - In deep sleep mode, the CPU and the DTC, ROM, and RAM clocks stop.
*                Peripheral functions do not stop.
*                
*                SW STANDBY - In this mode, the CPU, on-chip peripheral functions, and the oscillator functions stop.
*                However, the contents of the CPU internal registers, RAM data, on-chip peripheral functions,
*                and the states of the I/O ports are retained.
*                
* Note         : Smart Configurator is used to generate code for this sample program.
*                The Smart Configurator details and version number are given in the file 
*                header for each relevant module.
*
* Operating Conditions:
*                External Device/Component
*                -------------------------
*                Fit J8 and remove R164. Refer to section 3.3 of the RSKRX660 
*                User Manual (r20ut5017eg0100-rskrx660-usermanual.pdf) for details on component placement.
*                Connect an ammeter across J8, configure the ammeter for current measurement.
*
*                NOTE: If the ammeter is in a low current range then the ammeter's resistance may not allow the board
*                to function correctly.
*
*                NOTE: Use of the ammeter autorange function may cause large resistive loads to be
*                inserted in the power supply to the microcontroller, causing CPU under voltage on the supply rails
*                and a reset when the MCU comes out of SLEEP mode. For this reason, do not use the ammeter autorange
*                function and be sure to switch the ammeter back to the mA range before coming out of SLEEP mode.
*
* Operating Procedure:
*             1. Fit the PMOD LCD display to the board.  Ensure all the pins of the connector are correctly inserted 
*                in the socket.
*
*             2. Connect the E2 Lite Debugger to a free USB port on your computer. Connect the E2 Lite Debugger to the 
*                target hardware ensuring that it is plugged into the connector marked 'E2 Lite'.
*
*             3. Compile and download the sample code. Click the 'Go'button to start program execution.
*                Press 'Stop'and 'Disconects from debug tool...', disconnect the debugger from the RSK. 
*
*             4. Connect the 5V power on the RSK to launch the program.
*
*             5. The hardware platform and sample name will be displayed in the first two lines of the LCD display.
*                A real time clock will be displayed in the format: HH:MM:SS on the 3rd line of the LCD display.
*                'Normal Mode' will be displayed on the 4th line of the LCD display.
*                The clock is updated when the device is in normal operating mode.
*
*             6. The sample code will cycle through the different operating modes on each 
*                switch press starting with Normal, SLEEP, Normal, SW STANDBY, Normal then DEEP SW STANDBY. 
*
*             7. Press SW1 to enter SLEEP mode, 'Sleep Mode' will be displayed on the LCD. (*1)
*                Observe the current for SLEEP mode.  Press SW1 again to return to Normal mode.
*
*             8. Press SW1 to enter SW STANDBY mode, 'SW Standby Mode' will be displayed on the LCD. (*1)
*                Observe the current for SW STANDBY mode.  Press SW1 again to return to Normal mode.
*
*             9. Press SW1 to enter DEEP SW STANDBY mode, 'Deep SW Standby Mode' will be displayed on the LCD. (*1)
*                Observe the current for DEEP SW STANDBY mode. 
*
*            10. Press RESET to exit DEEP SW STANDBY  mode and go to step 7 to repeat the test.
*
*           (*1) To achieve accurate current measurements, please perform the following steps.
*
*             a. Disconnect 5V power from the RSK.
*
*             b. Disconnect the PMOD LCD display and connect 5V power to the RSK.
*
*             Current Reading after removing the PMOD LCD display:
*
*                NORMAL         : 16.5 mAmp
*                SLEEP          :  9.0 mAmp
*                SW STANDBY     :  0.8 mAmp
*                DEEP SW STANDBY: 10.5 uAmp
*
*                NOTE: Note that these values are approximate, not guaranteed.
*
***********************************************************************************************************************/
